ARC PUMP — 3-MINUTE PITCH SCRIPT (read aloud, share screen: arcpump.com/pay)
Speak a little slower than normal. Pause at each line break. Confidence > accent.

------------------------------------------------------------------
[0:00 — HOOK  ~15s]  (screen at the top of arcpump.com/pay)

Hi everyone, I'm Hau, and this is Arc Pump.
AI agents are starting to do real work.
But there is one thing they still can't do on their own — pay for it.

------------------------------------------------------------------
[0:15 — PROBLEM  ~20s]

When an agent needs an API, or data, or compute, it has to pay.
But today's payments are built for humans — subscriptions, API keys, checkout with a card.
None of that fits a machine that makes one call, once, with no human in the loop.

------------------------------------------------------------------
[0:35 — SOLUTION  ~20s]

Arc Pump is the missing piece: a pay-per-call payment rail for agents.
An agent pays per request, in USDC on Arc — metered, capped, and settled on-chain.
Think "x402" — but real, and live. Let me show you.

------------------------------------------------------------------
[0:55 — LIVE DEMO  ~75s]   *** this is the star — go slow, point at things ***
(scroll to "Don't take our word for it" and click  Run it → )

I'll click "Run it."
[Step 1 lights up] The agent calls a paid service and gets a 402 — Payment Required — with an invoice.
[Step 2] Its Circle wallet pays that invoice on Arc, in native USDC.
[the transaction hash appears] And there it is — a real transaction.
(click the "your tx / view tx" link) This is on Arcscan. A real payment, on-chain.
(come back to the page) The service verified it and returned the data.
And look — (scroll to the live ledger) it just appeared in the live ledger. Every paid call is here, verified on-chain.
(scroll to the budget simulator, drag a slider until it says "Blocked")
And it's bounded. Every agent has a per-call cap and a budget.
Push the price too high — and it refuses to pay. A guardrail, not a bug.

------------------------------------------------------------------
[2:10 — WHY IT MATTERS  ~15s]

This is the missing rail for the agent economy.
It's built on Circle Wallets and native USDC on Arc — safe signing, one-second settlement.
And it's open — anyone can plug in their own agent or service.

------------------------------------------------------------------
[2:45 — CLOSE  ~15s]

Arc Pump — agents paying for what they use.
It's live right now, at arcpump dot com slash pay.
Thank you.
------------------------------------------------------------------

BEFORE YOU PITCH — CHECKLIST:
- Do ONE warm-up "Run it" ~30 seconds before you go on, so it's ready.
- Then DON'T click again for at least ~20 seconds — there's a 15s cooldown; clicking too soon shows a rate-limit message live.
- Have the "Run it" section already on screen so you don't scroll around.
- Keep ArcPump-deck.pdf open in another tab as backup.
- Read this whole script out loud 3–4 times and time yourself. Aim to finish by 3:00.
