1. Challenge Explanation
I built under the Freestyle challenge. Arc Pump is agentic payment infrastructure with real-world impact: as AI agents start doing real work, they need to pay for the services they consume — APIs, data, compute — autonomously, which today's subscriptions and human checkout can't do. Arc Pump gives agents a pay-per-call rail: they pay per request in USDC on Arc, metered and bounded. That's the bootcamp's "real-world impact" applied to the emerging agent economy.

2. Submission Details
Arc Pump is a working pay-per-call payment rail for AI agents, live on Arc.
- Flow: an agent hits a paywalled service, gets 402 Payment Required plus an invoice, its wallet pays the invoice in native USDC through an on-chain PaymentRouter, then the service verifies the payment on-chain and serves the response (x402-style).
- I built all three sides: a Solidity PaymentRouter contract on Arc (it binds each payment to an invoice and recipient, since native USDC has no memo field), a service wrapper that issues the 402s and verifies on-chain, and a pay-client SDK with per-call and total-budget guardrails so an agent can't overspend.
- It is live and interactive at arcpump.com/pay: anyone can click "Run it" and watch a real AI agent pay 0.01 USDC on Arc to unlock a resource — step by step, with the on-chain transaction — or connect a wallet and pay it themselves. A live ledger shows every paid call.
- Signing is via Circle Programmable Wallets (MPC — no raw private key), and USDC is native on Arc (final in about 1 second). Built with Next.js and Cloudflare Workers, AI-assisted throughout.
- Key achievement: a genuinely autonomous, on-chain, verifiable agent payment — click a button on the web and a real stablecoin payment settles on Arc, with no human in the loop.
