The problem
AI agents can’t pay for what they use.
Agents are exploding — and they increasingly need to buy things on their own: APIs, data, compute, tools. But today’s payment models were built for humans:
  • Subscriptions & API keys don’t fit a machine that makes one call, once.
  • Cards & human checkout need a person in the loop — the opposite of autonomy.
  • There’s no clean, metered, per-request rail for agent-to-service payments.
2
The solution
Pay-per-call, in USDC on Arc.
An x402-style rail: an agent hits a paid resource, gets a 402 Payment Required + an invoice, and its wallet pays that invoice in native USDC through an on-chain router. The service verifies the payment on-chain, then serves the response.
  • Metered — pay exactly per request, down to a cent.
  • Bounded — every agent runs under a per-call cap and a total budget.
  • Verifiable — every payment is a real, invoice-bound tx on Arc.
3
How it works
Three steps.
01 · Request
402 + invoice
Agent calls the resource; the service replies with a price, an invoice id, and where to pay.
02 · Pay
router.pay(...)
The agent’s Circle wallet pays the invoice in native USDC through the on-chain PaymentRouter — within its caps.
03 · Unlock
verified → 200
The service checks the router in one view call (invoice + recipient bound), then serves the data. One payment, one call.

Built on
The stack.
  • PaymentRouter — a small Solidity contract on Arc that binds each payment to an invoice + recipient (native USDC has no memo). 0x42bCE0…28ff
  • Circle Programmable Wallets (MPC) — agents sign autonomously; no raw private key on any server.
  • Native USDC on Arc — payments are plain value transfers, final in ~1 second.
  • Next.js + Cloudflare Workers — the landing, the service wrapper (402), and the pay-client SDK.
